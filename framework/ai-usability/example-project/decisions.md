**Status:** Superseded

## DEC-002 — Explore a choice of collection timing

**Status:** Agreed  
**Decision date:** 2026-11-12  
**Decision maker:** Fictional service owner  
**Related record IDs:** DEC-001; FND-001; UN-002; PROP-001; PRT-001 v2  
**Superseded by:**

### Context

FND-001 records differing collection-timing preferences in the first research session.

### Choice

Select the direction represented by PRT-001 v2: offer a choice between a fixed 30-minute slot and a wider two-hour collection window.

Agree NFR-001: borrowers must be able to choose collection timing using only a keyboard. Agreement does not mean the requirement has passed verification.

### Rationale and alternatives

This preserves the option to plan around fixed commitments without assuming that every borrower needs the same precision. The former universal fixed-slot direction is superseded.

### Consequences

Check feasibility with service staff and test the revised design. Agreement selects a design direction; it does not establish implementation, rollout or live availability.

### Revisit or replacement

Revisit after operational feasibility work and the next research round. No replacement decision is recorded.
