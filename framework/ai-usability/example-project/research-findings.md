### Finding

The two participants wanted to understand collection arrangements before travelling, but preferred different levels of timing precision. PAR-001 wanted an exact time; PAR-002 preferred a wider window.

### Supporting evidence

- EV-001 in evidence.xlsx: PAR-001's statement at 00:03:10 in the corrected SES-001 transcript v02.
- EV-002 in evidence.xlsx: PAR-002's statement at 00:05:20 in the same transcript version.

### Context and limitations

This was one fictional moderated session with two fictional borrowers, reviewing PRT-001 v1. It identifies differing needs within that session; it does not establish how common either preference is among all borrowers. The v01 transcript contains a documented negation error and must not be substituted for v02.

### Implications

Explore collection options that can accommodate both preferences. Whether those options are operationally feasible needs further work. Any subsequent design choice belongs in the decision record and is not additional research evidence.
