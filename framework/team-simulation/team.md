# Fictional team and service

This cast is invented for the role-play. Behaviours are situational, not stereotypes or diagnoses of professions. Each person brings useful expertise and has legitimate pressures. One person could cover several of these contributions in a smaller service; this is not a mandatory staffing model.

The fictional council service lends community-event equipment. Its experience spans information, telephone and online enquiries, reservations, depot preparation, collection, returns and support. The starting brief proposes a booking app; discovery must establish what problem actually needs solving.

The team uses SharePoint for its shared working files. Following Miles's instruction, simultaneous editing is assumed resolved; the role-play does not treat it as an outstanding risk. This scenario choice does not prescribe a technology stack for the reusable template.

Illustrative user circumstances include caring responsibilities and uncertain travel, screen-reader use, limited digital confidence, telephone support, and the needs of staff providing the service. They are prompts for coverage, not validated personas or claims that these groups behave alike.

## Continuity and scope

This is a separate fictional project from the smaller AI-usability fixture. The role-play uses normal record prefixes beginning at 101 for shared/discovery records, 201 for alpha records and 301 for beta decisions/findings. Record states described in the phase reports are simulated; they have not been inserted into the blank working template.

The shared candidate needs concern understanding collection arrangements (UN-101), arranging borrowing through an accessible supported route (UN-102), and understanding cancellation and return responsibilities (UN-103). Their evidential and fulfilment states develop in the stories; candidate status is not proof of a need or its fulfilment.

The AI is a tool used by this team. It has no independent vote, line-management authority or permission to publish, contact people or decide scope. People perform the critical thinking, research analysis, synthesis and interpretation. After user testing, the researcher and team discuss observations, contradictions and explanations and reach their conclusions together. AI can then tidy the supplied conclusions, check exact source passages, flag discrepancies for human judgement and save authorised records without changing their meaning. It can also retrieve records, calculate defined totals and make routine authorised structural repairs.

Raw transcripts alone do not authorise AI-derived themes, findings, needs or conclusions; marking such work Draft would not make it human-led. Human analysis can be established from the supplied discussion, notes or existing records, without a separate approval register. This explicit sequence was added in the 0.3.1 revision; the cast and service remain fictional.
