## Answer from the project records

1. Establish the working folder and scope. For work in the project folder, use the [document tracker](../../document-tracker.xlsx) to find owning records and relevant additional files. Treat files under `how-to/` and `AI-skills/` as guidance or examples unless the task explicitly asks to use them as example evidence. [AI guidance](../../ai-guidance.md#start-with-the-task)
2. Read the relevant full rows, including [relevant extra columns](../../ai-guidance.md#read-and-preserve-extra-workbook-columns), and resolve exact IDs, versions and replacements. Check **Pending changes** in project config for relevant unsaved changes to current records. When a match is missing in the table, check the owning sheet for rows outside it before concluding the record is absent. Trace claims to the underlying source where accessible. Check what the records establish about proposals, decisions and actual delivery. [Record conventions](../../how-to/record-conventions.md#canonical-relationships)
3. Give a direct answer with the owning records, source locations and gaps that affect it. Say whether you checked the underlying source or a reference. If raw research could answer the question, locate relevant observations and leave interpretation to the team. For fulfilment questions, distinguish the team’s recorded assessment from the current scope and flag relevant changes or unassessed scope. For research coverage check actual roles in each session. For withdrawn FND or EV records, [trace direct and dependent claims](../../how-to/record-conventions.md#trace-withdrawn-evidence), including Answered questions linked from the finding. Explain the permitted reason for withdrawal and any current claim requiring review. Follow the [status and save rules](../../ai-guidance.md#check-status-and-what-was-saved), including when using the output of another skill. [User needs guide](../../how-to/files/user-needs.md)

When asked for advice, use the [guidance for experienced teams](../../ai-guidance.md#advise-an-experienced-team). Compare the options against the user's goal and the strongest evidence for and against each. Explain which option you recommend, its trade-off and what could change your view. Cite professional guidance separately from project evidence when it affects the advice.
