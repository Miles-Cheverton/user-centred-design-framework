# Prepare a briefing

## Copy-and-paste example

> Use AI-skills/prepare-a-briefing/SKILL.md with how-to/worked-service-handoff.md. Write a one-page handover for the service owner in this fictional example. Cover what the team agreed about collection messages, what it learnt on each route and what remains open.

## Prepare the brief

Confirm the audience, purpose and working folder. Use [Find and explain](../find-and-explain/SKILL.md#answer-from-the-project-records) to trace what people learnt or decided. Give their agreed priorities and criteria with their basis. Identify simulated behaviour, the scope of any fulfilment or verification result and open choices. [AI guidance](../../ai-guidance.md)

| Briefing | Include |
|---|---|
| Refinement | Proposed work, need, outcome, agreed criteria and open choices |
| Playback | Team findings, evidence limits and agreed or proposed next steps |
| Phase review | Key decisions, findings that support or challenge each one, remaining uncertainty and the next decision |
| Handover | Current decisions, exact design versions, key sources and recorded owners and next steps |

Return the brief in the requested format with source citations the audience can follow.
