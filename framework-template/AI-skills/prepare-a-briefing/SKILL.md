## Prepare the brief

Confirm the audience, purpose and working folder. For work across the project, use the [document tracker](../../document-tracker.xlsx) to find relevant records and additional files. Keep fictional examples out of live briefings. For a current-position brief, check approved meeting sources, any handover the team supplied and relevant **Pending changes** in project config. If there is reason to expect a decision is missing from the records, ask for the relevant source or report the limit. Use [Find and explain](../find-and-explain/SKILL.md#answer-from-the-project-records) to trace what people learnt or decided. Give their agreed priorities and criteria with their basis. Mention withdrawn findings or observations when they explain changes or leave gaps. [Trace affected claims and research questions](../../how-to/record-conventions.md#trace-withdrawn-evidence). Say what needs review and distinguish remaining evidence from team reassessment. Where relevant, give research coverage based on the roles and circumstances actually represented in each session. Use the [status and save rules](../../ai-guidance.md#check-status-and-what-was-saved) to label open or unsaved items. If using an unsaved update, cite the underlying source and say the register update is pending. Identify simulated behaviour, the exact scope of any fulfilment or verification result and whether it applies to the current version. Distinguish earlier test results from unassessed or changed scope, and identify the team’s open choices. [AI guidance](../../ai-guidance.md)

| Briefing | Include |
|---|---|
| Refinement | Proposed work, need, outcome, agreed criteria and open choices |
| Playback | Team findings, evidence limits and next steps the team proposed or agreed |
| Phase review | Key decisions, findings that support or challenge each one, remaining uncertainty and decisions the team has left open |
| Handover | Current decisions, exact design versions, key sources and recorded owners and next steps |

Use the [guidance for experienced teams](../../ai-guidance.md#advise-an-experienced-team) to choose what matters to the audience. For a release decision, put the user outcome beside what the team tested, what actually shipped, the effect on staff or other channels, and any dependency that could change the decision. Give an owner or review date when recorded. For a recommendation, distinguish the assistant's advice from the team's agreed position.

Return the brief in the requested format with source citations the audience can follow.
