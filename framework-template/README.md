## Why the framework exists

Keep research findings, user needs and decisions easy to find and check. **People think first. AI helps second.** Give the team time to form its own ideas and interpret the evidence. Then use the assistant to find sources, flag gaps and record what the team agrees.

## Use the files

Use a workbook when you have something to record. Replace its **OVERWRITE ME** row with the first real entry. All other starter rows, folders and worked examples are illustrative. See [how to add records](how-to/record-conventions.md#edit-records). Complete relevant bracketed prompts in Markdown. The folders named RND-001 and JNY-001 show how to store a research round or journey version. Record [prototypes](how-to/files/prototypes.md) by linking to the exact version.

Record substantive learning and decisions during the team’s normal work. Link to exact versions of supporting material in approved tools. Keep participant identities, recordings and transcripts in restricted storage under the [research protocol](how-to/research-protocol.md).

For AI assistance, share the [project config](project-config.md), [AI guidance](ai-guidance.md), the relevant [skill](AI-skills/README.md) and only the records the tool is approved to process.
