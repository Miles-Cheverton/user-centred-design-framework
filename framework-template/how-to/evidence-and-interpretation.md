# Evidence and interpretation

Use observations and exact sources to show how the team reached a conclusion and where it applies.

| Record | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Source | Material the team received or found. [Check what it can support](files/source-register.md#decide-how-much-weight-to-give-a-claim). |
| Observation (EV) | What someone said, did or measured, in context. |
| Finding (FND) | What the team concluded from the evidence. |
| Assumption | A belief the team is working with while it checks the evidence. |
| Need | What a user needs to achieve and why. |
| Decision | What the team chose and why. |

## Human analysis before documentation

1. After testing, bring the researcher, observers and relevant colleagues together. Compare what people saw and heard, discuss explanations and consider counterexamples.
2. Agree what the evidence supports. Keep open questions, limits and disagreements in the team’s notes.
3. Write the team’s conclusions in [Findings](files/findings-and-observations.md). Include substantive learning that challenges the preferred option or remains inconclusive.

Once the team has reached its conclusions, AI can tidy their wording, find exact passages, check citations and flag discrepancies against authorised sources. Take discrepancies that could change the meaning back to the team. With raw material alone, AI can locate requested observations or extracts. People still do the interpretation and decide what to record as a finding.

For example, the team concludes that some borrowers mistake a reservation for permission to travel. If an extract shows that others understood the distinction, retain that difference. If the draft says “all borrowers”, ask the team to review the claim.

## Make evidence checkable

Use **Evidence and source** in [Findings](../findings-and-observations.xlsx) to put supporting observations beside their sources. Give the session or source ID (SES or DOC), exact file or version, and timestamp, page, section or range. Use a separate EV row when an observation needs its own citation. [Register non-session sources](files/source-register.md).

A recording citation might be `SES-003`, `2026-11-16_RND-001_SES-003_screen_p01.mp4`, 00:12:45–00:13:20. Say what was observed and give the method, group and limits that affect the claim.

## Check a claim

1. Identify the claim, the decision it affects and the users, conditions and version it covers.
2. Follow referenced findings to the underlying sources. Check when and how the evidence was gathered, whether it supports the claim and whether other evidence changes the picture.
3. Say what you checked and what you could access. Explain how any gap affects the decision. Point to the source or question that would help the team resolve it.

If evidence changes, follow it to the affected findings, needs and decisions so the team can review them. [AI guidance](../ai-guidance.md) covers workbook access and the limits of the assistant’s role.
