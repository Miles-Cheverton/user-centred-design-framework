# Dependencies

**Working file** [dependencies.xlsx](../../dependencies.xlsx)

## First capture

Use a DEP ID and name what the project needs another party or system to provide.

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| ID | Stable DEP ID. |
| Dependency | What is needed and who or what should provide it. |
| Needed by | The date or milestone, if known. |
| Owner | The person or role who will confirm the agreement and follow it through. |
| Status | Unconfirmed, Agreed, In progress, Available, Blocked or No longer needed. Use Available when delivery or access is confirmed. |

Check the owner and status before relying on the dependency.

**Example** The identity platform team needs to provide a test environment before integration testing. The technical lead owns the dependency and is waiting for confirmation.
