# Stakeholders

**Working file** [stakeholders.xlsx](../../stakeholders.xlsx)

## First capture

Enter an **ID** and **Name** for a person or team with an interest in, influence over or responsibility for the service.

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| ID | Stable stakeholder ID, for example `STK-001`. |
| Name | Person or team name. Use the team name while the contact is unknown. |
| Job title | Position, when known. |
| Email | Work address, where appropriate. |
| Why involved | Their responsibility or interest and organisation where useful. For example, “Casework handles applications sent for manual review.” |

Update contacts and responsibilities when they change. Cite stakeholder input for the context or decision it provides. Use [research evidence](../evidence-and-interpretation.md) to support claims about user needs.

[GDS guidance on mapping the user’s whole problem](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/map-a-users-whole-problem)
