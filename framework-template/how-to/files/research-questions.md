# Research questions

**Working file** [research-questions.xlsx](../../research-questions.xlsx)

## First capture

Enter an **ID** and **Question**. Write a question the research can answer, such as “How do applicants currently find out when a decision is likely?”

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| ID | Stable research question ID, for example `RQ-001`. |
| Question | What the team needs to learn. Leave room for an unexpected answer. |
| Decision informed | What the team will decide or understand using the answer. |
| Status | Open, Investigating, Answered or Superseded. Mark Answered when a team-reviewed finding supports the answer. |
| Superseded by | Exact replacement RQ IDs. Flag an unknown replacement. |

Plan to answer the question in time for the decision. Cite the RQ ID in **Evidence and source** in [findings](../../findings-and-observations.xlsx), including any limits on the answer.

[GDS guidance on planning user research](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/plan-user-research-for-your-service)
