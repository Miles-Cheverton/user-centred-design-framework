# Frequently asked questions

## Getting started

### Do I need to use every file?

No. Use the records your project needs and add others as they become useful. [Start with your work](README.md#frame-the-work).

### Why are there separate records?

Each type of information has a clear home. Links and IDs help people and AI assistants find the relevant records and understand how they relate.

### Will the framework create more administration?

The aim is to record useful information during activities the team already carries out. An assistant can check sources, find existing records and draft updates after people agree the content.

### Who maintains the records?

The team maintains them during its work. Decide together where the current records live and who can make or approve changes.

### Can we use this on an existing project?

Yes. Identify the current records and locations, then use [Import an existing project](../AI-skills/import-existing-project/SKILL.md) to help bring relevant information together.

### Can we keep using tools like SharePoint, Figma or a delivery backlog?

Yes. Link to the exact records and versions in your approved tools. Use the [tracker and local conventions](README.md#adapt-the-starter) to show where the team maintains the current record.

### Can we rename files or add columns?

Yes. Keep the tracker and links current, use clear column headings and preserve existing IDs. See [how to adapt the starter](README.md#adapt-the-starter).

## What goes where?

### What is the difference between user needs, use cases, business rules and requirements?

For a repairs service, a need might be knowing the confirmed appointment so the tenant can be at home. A use case describes rearranging the appointment. A business rule sets the conditions for changing it. A functional requirement describes a specific capability, such as allowing eligible tenants to choose a new slot. A non-functional requirement describes a testable service quality, such as the time taken to display available slots. [Design and development](design-and-development.md).

### What is the difference between the document tracker and the source register?

The tracker points to current project records and useful files. The source register identifies exact sources that support claims or need their versions tracked. [File a source](research-and-evidence.md#file-a-source).

### Where do other project documents belong?

Keep them in the workspace where they are useful and add relevant links to the tracker. Register a source when a record needs to cite it.

### Why does each record need an ID?

A stable ID lets decisions, evidence and needs refer to the same record even when files move or wording changes. [Record conventions](../AI-skills/record-conventions.md#stable-identifiers-and-owner-lookup).

### Should we record every team meeting?

Record a meeting when the detail or reasoning will help the team revisit a significant choice. Use the shared delivery board for routine actions and blockers. [Meeting follow-up](research-and-evidence.md#capture-what-changed-in-a-meeting).

### What if evidence or a decision changes?

Keep the original ID and permitted history. Record why the team changed its position and review the records affected. [Withdrawal](research-and-evidence.md#withdraw-a-finding) and [Decisions](project-setup-and-management.md#decisions).

## Working with AI

### Can we use the framework without AI?

Yes. The records and guidance work on their own.

### Which AI assistant should we use?

Use an approved assistant that can read the relevant project files. The [skills setup guide](../AI-skills/README.md#set-up-your-ai-assistant) gives examples for different tools.

### Can AI analyse user research for us?

People discuss the evidence, form their own conclusions and decide what it supports. Afterwards, AI can check sources, organise the evidence and help document the team's conclusions. [Human analysis before documentation](research-and-evidence.md#human-analysis-before-documentation).

### How do we check an AI assistant's work?

Ask for the record IDs, exact source locations and evidence limits. Review proposed changes and verify what was saved. Use [Check evidence and records](../AI-skills/check-evidence-and-records/SKILL.md) for a wider check.

### What if the assistant has incomplete access?

Give it the records or approved extracts needed for the task. It should explain which sources it could check and where a conclusion remains uncertain.

### Why does each skill have its own folder?

The [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) uses a named folder with a SKILL.md file so compatible assistants can discover and load skills individually.
