# Research Questions

**Working file:** [research-questions.xlsx](../../research-questions.xlsx)

Identify what the team needs to learn and which decisions the answers will inform. Use to focus research on the uncertainties that affect real decisions.


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Question**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. Write one open, answerable question.
2. State the decision or understanding it will inform.
3. Prioritise according to the consequence and timing of that decision.
4. Record the answering or qualifying findings in research-findings.md, with this question in their Research question IDs field. Retrieve them by that field; do not maintain a second list here.

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable research-question ID. |
| Question | At capture | One answerable question; avoid embedding a preferred answer. |
| Decision informed | Before use | Why the answer matters and what it will help decide. |
| Priority | Before use | Relative investigation priority. Choices: `High`, `Medium`, `Low`. |
| Status | Before use | Current investigation state. Choices: `Open`, `Investigating`, `Answered`, `Superseded`. |
| Superseded by | When a replacement exists | Zero or more exact replacement RQ ID values of the same record type, separated by semicolon plus space. Required when a replacement exists. If Superseded but the replacement is unknown, leave blank and flag an unresolved reference gap; never invent a successor. No self-links or cycles. |

## Keep it current

Refine or supersede a question when the decision changes. An Answered question without a supporting finding in research-findings.md has an unresolved evidence gap. Answered does not mean uncertainty has disappeared; findings should preserve limits. List all replacements in Superseded by when a question is replaced.

## Completion check

The question does not merely seek confirmation of the team's preferred solution.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
