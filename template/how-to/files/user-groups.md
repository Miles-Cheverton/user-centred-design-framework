# User Groups

**Working file:** [user-groups.xlsx](../../user-groups.xlsx)

Describe identified groups who use, provide or experience the service. Use during discovery and whenever research reveals a materially different experience or circumstance.


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Name**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. Identify groups through their relationship to the service and relevant behaviours.
2. Include people who provide or support the service, and people facing access barriers.
3. Link evidence. When a description is provisional, say so in Description.

## Fictional example

This example demonstrates the format only. It is not project evidence and is not preloaded into the workbook. It shows a developed record; the first capture can be smaller. Empty cells mean not recorded.

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable group ID referenced by needs and research records. |
| Name | At capture | A short recognisable group name. |
| Description | Before use | Who belongs to the group and the relevant service context. |
| Relevant circumstances | When known or relevant | Behaviours, access barriers or support needs relevant to this work. |
| Supporting record IDs | When known or relevant | Zero or more EV or FND IDs supporting the group description, separated by semicolon plus space. Leave blank when the description is unverified; a blank is not evidence of absence. |

## Keep it current

Refine descriptions as evidence develops. Preserve IDs when the group remains the same; record how groups were split or combined if their boundaries change materially.

## Completion check

The description helps recruit or design for real people and avoids invented biographies or unnecessary personal characteristics.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
