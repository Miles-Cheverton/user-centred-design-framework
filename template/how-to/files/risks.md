# Risks

**Working file:** [risks.xlsx](../../risks.xlsx)

Record uncertain events that may affect outcomes and the response being taken. Use for an uncertain event that could materially affect outcomes or delivery.


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Risk statement**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. Write a cause → event → consequence statement.
2. Record controls already operating, separately from proposed actions.
3. Agree the assessment timeframe and Low/Medium/High criteria for this project before assigning ratings; use Not assessed until then.
4. Assign the next action, owner, due point and review date. Record acceptance or escalation decisions when needed.

This example demonstrates the format only. It is not project evidence and is not preloaded into the workbook. It shows a developed record; the first capture can be smaller. Empty cells mean not recorded.

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable risk ID. |
| Risk statement | At capture | Use cause → uncertain event → consequence in one clear sentence. |
| Current controls | Before use | Controls already operating; use 'None identified' if appropriate. |
| Current likelihood | Before use | Assessment under the project's stated criteria and timeframe. Choices: `Not assessed`, `Low`, `Medium`, `High`. |
| Current impact | Before use | Potential consequence under the project's stated criteria. Choices: `Not assessed`, `Low`, `Medium`, `High`. |
| Next action | Before use | The next treatment, acceptance or escalation action. |
| Owner | Before use | Person or role accountable for the risk. |
| Action due | When known or relevant | Date or milestone for the action. |
| Review date | When known or relevant | Next substantive risk review date. |
| Status | Before use | Current handling state. Choices: `Open`, `Accepted`, `Occurred`, `Closed`. |

## Keep it current

Review ratings after controls change or new evidence appears. A planned control does not reduce the current rating until implemented and checked. Accepted risks remain subject to review. If a risk occurs, arrange issue handling and record the actual event.

## Completion check

The statement is an uncertainty, the current rating reflects existing controls, and someone owns the response.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
