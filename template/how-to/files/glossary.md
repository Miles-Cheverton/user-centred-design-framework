# Glossary

**Working file:** [glossary.xlsx](../../glossary.xlsx)

Keep shared terminology and alternative names consistent. Use when a term has a specific meaning or several names could cause misunderstanding.


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Term**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. Choose the preferred term used in the files and team conversations.
2. Write a concise project-specific definition.
3. Add genuine alternative names or abbreviations.

## Fictional example

This example demonstrates the format only. It is not project evidence and is not preloaded into the workbook. It shows a developed record; the first capture can be smaller. Empty cells mean not recorded.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| ID | TERM-001 |
| Term | Research round |
| Definition | A planned set of research activities addressing related questions |
| Aliases | Study round; round |

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable term ID. |
| Term | At capture | The preferred term used in this project. |
| Definition | Before use | Its concise meaning in this project's context. |
| Aliases | When known or relevant | Other names or abbreviations people may search for. |

## Keep it current

Retain the ID when wording improves without changing the concept. Explain a material change in meaning and update affected records.

## Completion check

The definition resolves a real ambiguity and avoids defining a term only with other unexplained jargon.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
