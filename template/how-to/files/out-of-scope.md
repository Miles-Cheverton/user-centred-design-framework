# Out Of Scope

**Working file:** [out-of-scope.xlsx](../../out-of-scope.xlsx)

Record the current agreed exclusions and the reasons for them. Use to make exclusions explicit, including exclusions that stakeholders might otherwise assume are included.


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Item**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. Name the excluded area specifically.
2. Explain the reason without implying the area has no user value.
3. Link the agreement and check that the same item is not also in scope.

This example demonstrates the format only. It is not project evidence and is not preloaded into the workbook. It shows a developed record; the first capture can be smaller. Empty cells mean not recorded.

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable scope-item ID; keep it if the item moves between scope lists. |
| Item | At capture | A clearly bounded piece of work, population, channel or service area. |
| Reason | Before use | Why the item is included or excluded. |
| Decision ID | When known or relevant | One DEC ID for the agreed decision establishing this boundary, for example DEC-001. Leave blank if no decision is recorded; do not enter prose or a URL here. |

## Keep it current

Keep the same ID if an item moves into scope. Record the decision and remove it from this current list.

## Completion check

The exclusion and its reason are understandable without an oral explanation.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
