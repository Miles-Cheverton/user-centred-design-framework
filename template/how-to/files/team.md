# Team

**Working file:** [team.xlsx](../../team.xlsx)

Make the people doing the work and their project responsibilities clear. Use when establishing the team, onboarding people or changing responsibilities.

Critical thinking, research analysis and service decisions are human responsibilities. The researcher supports observers and relevant colleagues to analyse evidence together; AI can then help check sources and document their conclusions. Involve people who perform critical service steps, including operations and supported channels, through the team's existing activities. See [human analysis before documentation](../evidence-and-interpretation.md#human-analysis-before-documentation).


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Name**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. Record the person or team carrying out each responsibility.
2. Use Vacant where a necessary role is unfilled.
3. Describe the responsibility in project terms; formal job titles may differ from project roles.

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable team-member or team-role record ID. |
| Name | At capture | Person or team; use 'Vacant' for an unfilled role. |
| Job title | When known or relevant | The person's formal title. |
| Email | When known or relevant | Work contact address, when appropriate. |
| Project responsibility | Before use | What this person or team is responsible for in this project. |

## Keep it current

Keep the current directory accurate as people join, leave or change role. The decision record names the person responsible for each actual decision.

## Completion check

Every current responsibility has a named person/team or an explicit vacancy.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
