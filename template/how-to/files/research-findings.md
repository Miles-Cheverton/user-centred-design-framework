# Research findings

**Working file:** [research-findings.md](../../research-findings.md)

A finding is a conclusion reached by people through analysis of evidence. The researcher and team discuss observations and interpretations first; AI may then tidy and source-check their conclusions and record them faithfully. Use one section per substantive finding, including contradictory and inconclusive ones. Do not paste raw transcripts into this file or ask AI to generate findings from them.


## First capture

Save a stable FND ID, the concise human conclusion and Draft status. Capture every substantive finding, including contradictory, unexpected and inconclusive learning. Draft is a documentation state, not permission for AI-authored interpretation. Complete the source basis and context before relying on it.

## Complete a finding

1. The researcher and team review observations together, looking for patterns, differences and contrary evidence. Discuss alternative explanations before reaching conclusions; follow [human analysis before documentation](../evidence-and-interpretation.md#human-analysis-before-documentation).
2. State the conclusion people reached, including uncertainty or unresolved disagreement. Existing analysis notes or a supplied team account can establish this; no new approval record is required.
3. Attach precise evidence references and the limits of the conclusion. AI may check against authorised transcripts and tidy wording, but any meaning-changing discrepancy goes back to the team.
4. Check that the record preserves the human analysis before marking Reviewed. Review attribution refers to actual human review, not an AI consistency check.
5. Record the question, round/session and exact design versions this finding concerns. Needs, proposals and decisions cite the finding from their owning records; do not copy inverse lists.

## Fictional example

**FND-001 — Applicants seek an expected decision time**

After discussing their observations, the team concluded that applicants in the example sessions looked for information about when to expect a decision. AI checked the supplied conclusion against EV-001 and EV-002 and helped document it. The team retained the small-sample limitation and decided to investigate what timing information can be supplied reliably. AI did not derive the finding from the transcript.

## Fields and sections

Use the small first capture to save a draft. Complete the relevant fields before relying on it; an unfinished record does not establish evidence, agreement or verification.

| Field or section | Needed when | What belongs here |
|---|---|---|
| Finding ID and title | At capture | Stable FND ID and a short conclusion. |
| Research question IDs | When answering a recorded question | Exact RQ IDs; unexpected findings do not need an invented question. |
| User group IDs | When the finding concerns user groups | Exact UG IDs covered by the evidence. |
| Round or session IDs | When based on project research | Exact RND or SES IDs. |
| Design versions | When the finding concerns a design | Exact JNY or PRT IDs and versions; this file owns the finding-to-design link. |
| Status | At capture; update on substantive review | Draft, Reviewed or Superseded. Reviewed needs checked supporting evidence and review attribution. |
| Superseded by | When replacements exist | Exact replacement FND IDs separated by semicolons; blank on a Superseded finding is an unresolved gap. |
| Reviewed by and on | When Reviewed | Reviewer and actual substantive review date. |
| Finding | At capture | The conclusion in plain language, including an inconclusive result where warranted. |
| Supporting evidence | Before reliance or Reviewed | EV IDs and what they support; follow each to its exact Source ID, Source version or path and Locator. |
| Context and limitations | Before reliance or Reviewed | Coverage, circumstances, uncertainty and contradictory evidence. |
| Implications | When the learning changes what the team should consider | Explain the implication. Decisions own their references to findings; do not maintain DEC backlinks here. |

## Keep it current

After research or analysis, capture all substantive learning here. Revise a conclusion when new or contradictory evidence changes its meaning. Keep the ID when clarifying the same finding. Use Superseded and enter every exact replacement FND ID in Superseded by when the conclusion materially changes. Retain the original finding; do not create self-links or replacement cycles.

## Completion check

A reader can separate the human conclusion from its observations, locate the evidence and understand how far the finding applies. AI assistance has not added themes, implications or certainty the team did not establish.

## Practice basis

[GDS: analysing research](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/analyse-a-research-session) and [sharing findings](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/sharing-user-research-findings). The record structure is our implementation choice. Guidance reviewed: 16 November 2026.
