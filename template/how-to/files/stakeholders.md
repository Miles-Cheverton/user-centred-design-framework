# Stakeholders

**Working file:** [stakeholders.xlsx](../../stakeholders.xlsx)

Identify people and organisations with an interest in, influence over or responsibility for the service. Use when identifying who can supply context, affect decisions or help deliver and operate the service.


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Name**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. Identify the person or team and their organisation.
2. Record only appropriate contact information.
3. Explain the stakeholder's actual interest or responsibility; a job title alone is insufficient.

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable stakeholder ID. |
| Name | At capture | The person's name or the named team while the contact is unknown. |
| Job title | When known or relevant | Their organisational position. |
| Organisation | When known or relevant | The organisation they represent. |
| Email | When known or relevant | Their work contact address, when appropriate. |
| Interest or responsibility | Before use | Why this stakeholder is relevant to the service. |

## Keep it current

Update contacts and responsibilities when they change. Avoid treating a senior stakeholder's opinion as user research evidence.

## Completion check

A new team member can understand why to involve this stakeholder.
