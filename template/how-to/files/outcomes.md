# Outcomes

**Working file:** [outcomes.xlsx](../../outcomes.xlsx)

Describe the changes the service intends to achieve, independently of proposed features. Use when agreeing why the service exists and when testing whether a proposed change serves that purpose.


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Outcome**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. Describe a change in users' or the organisation's circumstances.
2. Identify who benefits and link relevant needs.
3. Use success-measures.md to define how the change will be assessed.

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable outcome ID. |
| Outcome | At capture | A plain statement of the intended change or benefit. |
| Beneficiary IDs | Before use | One or more UG or STK IDs identifying who benefits, separated by semicolon plus space. Do not enter names or descriptions here. |
| User need IDs | When known or relevant | Zero or more UN IDs for directly related user needs, separated by semicolon plus space. Leave blank if no direct relationship has been established; do not invent a need. |

## Keep it current

Refine an outcome when understanding changes. Record a material change of intent in decisions.md and retain traceability to its previous meaning.

## Completion check

The wording describes a benefit, rather than a deliverable such as 'launch an app'.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
