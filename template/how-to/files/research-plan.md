# Research Plan

**Working file:** [research-plan.xlsx](../../research-plan.xlsx)

Plan focused rounds of research and link each round to its executable protocol. Use to organise the next useful research rounds and review their progress.


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Research question IDs**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. Select the questions and relevant groups.
2. Choose a method suited to the questions and available evidence.
3. Assign a lead and complete the linked round protocol before marking Ready.
4. Add actual session records in research-sessions.xlsx when sessions take place; do not place long lists of recording links in this plan.

Plan the human analysis as part of the round: the researcher, observers and relevant team members discuss the observations and reach conclusions before AI helps check sources and document them. Use the protocol's existing Analysis and Close-out sections; see [the research protocol](../research-protocol.md#analyse-and-close-the-round).

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable round ID. |
| Research question IDs | At capture | One or more RQ IDs for the research questions this round addresses, separated by semicolon plus space. |
| User group IDs | The round includes user groups | Zero or more UG IDs for the groups included, separated by semicolon plus space. Required for research involving user groups; leave blank for studies without participants and explain that scope in the protocol. Do not put Not applicable in an ID field. |
| Method | Before use | The method chosen to answer the questions. |
| Planned date | When known or relevant | Planned start date, in YYYY-MM-DD form when known. |
| Lead | Before use | Research lead or responsible role. |
| Status | Before use | Current round state. Choices: `Proposed`, `Preparing`, `Ready`, `In progress`, `Analysing`, `Complete`, `Cancelled`. |
| Protocol path | Before use | Link to the round's protocol.md. |

## Keep it current

Revise upcoming rounds as learning changes priorities. Complete means reviewed findings are available to inform work, not just that sessions have finished. Find sessions by Round ID in research-sessions.xlsx and findings by Round or session IDs in research-findings.md; do not keep duplicate lists here.

Known substantive material still awaiting human analysis keeps the round Analysing. An unanswered question can remain as an inconclusive finding after analysis; do not invent a confident answer to close the round. Distinguish groups planned from those actually heard from using the existing round and session records.

## Completion check

Every planned round has a clear question, method, lead and executable protocol.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
