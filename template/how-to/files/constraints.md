# Constraints

**Working file:** [constraints.xlsx](../../constraints.xlsx)

Record established limits affecting the possible solution. Use when a real boundary affects what the team can change.


## First capture

Enter **ID**, **Constraint**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it.

## Complete a record

1. State the constraint and its actual source.
2. Explain its practical effect.
3. Choose Flexibility after checking what the project can change; use Unknown when changeability has not been established.
4. Record the authority or condition needed to change it.

## Fictional example

This example demonstrates the format only. It is not project evidence and is not preloaded into the workbook. It shows a developed record; the first capture can be smaller. Empty cells mean not recorded.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| ID | CON-001 |
| Constraint | Use the organisation's approved identity service |
| Source | DOC-002 |
| Effect | Identity checks must connect to an existing service |
| Flexibility | Negotiable |
| Change authority or condition | Architecture approval would be needed for an alternative |

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| ID | At capture | Stable constraint ID. |
| Constraint | At capture | The limit the team must take into account. |
| Source | Before use | Authority pointer in plain text: the applicable policy, document/version, agreement, decision or authoritative URL and its relevant section. May include a DOC or DEC ID, but this is not an ID-only relationship field. State any uncertainty about applicability. |
| Effect | Before use | How the constraint affects this service or project. |
| Flexibility | Before use | Whether this project could change it. Choices: `Fixed for this project`, `Negotiable`, `Unknown`. |
| Change authority or condition | When known or relevant | Who could approve a change or what would need to happen; state why fixed when relevant. |

## Keep it current

Reassess flexibility when authority, timescales or circumstances change. Record removal or renegotiation through decisions.md; do not silently leave an obsolete constraint operative.

## Completion check

The constraint has a source, and an unverified belief has not been presented as an established limit.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
