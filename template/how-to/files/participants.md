# Participants

**Working file:** [participants.xlsx](../../participants.xlsx)

Maintain a minimal coded participant register that links participation to user groups. Create a coded entry when a participant joins the research, before linking their sessions.


## First capture

Enter **Participant ID**, **User group IDs**. Keep unknowns visible; do not invent them. This saves a first record, not an established or complete position. Complete the applicable fields below before acting on or relying on it. Use participant codes only; keep names and contact details in the approved restricted system.

## Complete a record

1. Allocate an unused PAR ID; never derive it from a name, email or other personal identifier.
2. Link the relevant user-group IDs.
3. Record only research characteristics needed for coverage or interpretation. Keep recruitment contacts and the identity key separately restricted.

## Fields

The timing below separates quick capture from a usable record. **Before use** means before acting on or relying on the record. Specific conditions still apply; capture does not establish consent, evidence, agreement or verification. A blank means not recorded, not false, zero or none.

| Field | Needed when | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| Participant ID | At capture | Stable participant code; never use a name as the ID. |
| User group IDs | At capture | One or more UG IDs for the user groups represented, separated by semicolon plus space. |
| Relevant research characteristics | When known or relevant | Only characteristics necessary to understand recruitment coverage or findings. |

## Keep it current

Retain the code across repeat sessions where appropriate. Follow consent, withdrawal and retention arrangements; remove data when required rather than treating version history as permission to retain it.

## Completion check

The register has no names, email addresses or unnecessary personal details. Coded data remains potentially identifiable and requires suitable access controls.

Use [record conventions](../record-conventions.md) for stable IDs, references, dates and workbook editing. Permitted values and meanings are documented in [allowed-values.xlsx](../allowed-values.xlsx); dropdowns are already built into each workbook.
