# Answer a project question

## Supply

- **Question:** [The question to answer]
- **Scope:** [Relevant service area, user group, proposal, version or time period]
- **Desired depth:** [Brief answer / fuller explanation]

## Prompt

Follow ai-guidance.md and the project’s agreed terminology.

Retrieve and explain existing human conclusions; do not perform fresh research analysis. A raw transcript does not authorise you to infer themes, findings, needs or fulfilment. If no human conclusion is available in the supplied explanation, analysis notes or existing records, state that analysis by the team is needed. You may locate requested literal observations or flag a specific mismatch without deciding its interpretation.

Answer the supplied question from the relevant project records. Read README.md, ai-guidance.md and the canonical relationship reference in how-to/record-conventions.md, then the relevant file guides and records. Do not read every file indiscriminately.

Check the status, applicability and exact version of each material source. Distinguish observations, findings, assumptions, proposed designs and agreed decisions. Resolve exact typed IDs through the relationship reference; use ID plus Version for a journey or prototype. Follow explicit replacement references. Never invent or fuzzy-match a missing join.

For each relevant workbook, establish its actual sheet/table, headers and full relevant rows. Preserve IDs, status, versions and source paths in extracts. A partial preview cannot establish complete coverage or record absence. Exclude how-to examples, framework administration and the synthetic framework/ai-usability/ fixture from live project evidence.

Follow material claims through their supporting findings or evidence to the exact Source ID, Source version or path and Locator in evidence.xlsx. Distinguish an underlying source that was actually checked from an inaccessible source referenced by a record.

If you cannot access a necessary workbook, graphic, recording, transcript or prototype, state precisely what you could inspect and how the missing material limits the answer. Reading a source pointer does not establish that the source itself was read.

Find reverse relationships by querying their owning fields: findings hold Research question IDs, Round or session IDs and Design versions; prototypes hold Proposal IDs. For a round, include findings linked directly to its RND ID or through its SES records. Do not maintain duplicate backlinks. Include substantive contradictory and inconclusive findings, not only support for a preferred answer.

If the question concerns whether needs are met, distinguish user-needs.xlsx evidential Status from user-need-coverage.xlsx Assessment and Delivery decision. Validate assessed claims against EV support, date and exact version/scope. Reconcile each active UN to one current coverage row before claiming project-wide coverage; Summary counts are recorded-row counts across independent dimensions, not completeness or a percentage.

Return:

1. A direct answer in a short paragraph.
2. The few source references needed to check it, using file, actual sheet or section, record ID and exact version or locator where relevant.
3. Any missing or conflicting evidence that changes the answer.

Do not fill a missing human conclusion with an inference, even if labelled as a draft. Do not manufacture certainty, fill gaps with general knowledge or silently turn a proposal into a decision. If the answer is not established in the records or supplied human analysis, say so and identify the smallest useful question for the team or source check.

Do not edit project records unless separately instructed.

## Human check

Open the important references. Confirm that the answer preserves the source’s scope, date, status and meaning before relying on it in a decision.
