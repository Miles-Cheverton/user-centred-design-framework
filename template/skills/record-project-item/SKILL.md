# Record a project item

Follow the [shared skill rules](../README.md). Use a specialised skill for needs, use cases, findings, assumptions, decisions, design options or need coverage.

## Supported records

This procedure covers user groups, outcomes, stakeholders, team members, scope items, constraints, dependencies, risks, research questions/rounds/sessions, coded participants, business rules, non-functional requirements, glossary terms and supplied-document records. It does not invent a new record type or register.

## Procedure

1. Identify the intended concept and read its guide under how-to/files/. If classification changes the meaning—such as constraint versus assumption—resolve that distinction from the source or retain an explicit question.
2. Read the current owner and exact schema. Check for an existing matching record and stable ID. Use the guide’s small first capture, then complete the fields needed before the record is used.
3. Extract literal facts and the team's stated analysis or decisions, then resolve typed references through the canonical relationship table. Do not derive user groups, outcomes, risks or conclusions by synthesising raw research. Human judgement supplies their meaning; AI may check, tidy and record it. Keep narrative authority citations distinct from ID-only fields.
4. Apply the concept’s particular conditions: scope belongs in one current scope list; a negotiable constraint needs its change condition; participant research needs applicable consent; a rule needs its authority; requirement agreement and verification are separate. Do not infer any of these from a document title or senior speaker alone.
5. Save the bounded record when authorised and verify its references. Preserve unrelated rows, file controls and meaningful history.

## Result

Return the owner file, record ID and what changed, plus unresolved facts affecting use. Do not send messages, create commitments, collect unnecessary personal information or start research as a side effect of recording an item.
