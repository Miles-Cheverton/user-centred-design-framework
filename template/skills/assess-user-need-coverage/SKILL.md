# Assess user-need coverage

Follow the [shared skill rules](../README.md) and the [coverage guide](../../how-to/files/user-need-coverage.md). Read the actual user-needs and user-need-coverage tables; use the current guide for their exact columns and choices.

## Procedure

1. Establish the service/design version and user circumstances being assessed. Reconcile the relevant active UN records with the coverage table. Preserve proposed needs as proposed; do not make the need itself Evidenced by assessing a design against it.
2. Establish the human assessment and its scope from the team or existing records, then check the cited evidence and exact version. Do not choose Not met, Partly met or Met in full by interpreting research yourself. Use Not assessed when a human assessment has not been established; return the relevant records and explicit gaps for the team's judgement.
3. Record the team's remaining gap and flag discrepancies between the assessment and its recorded basis. A passed component check or one participant's success does not establish whole-service fulfilment. For example, a passing web form and a failing telephone route must be considered together by the team; AI must not turn the component pass into Met in full.
4. Record the separate delivery decision from an actual decision: whether to address, defer or not address the need. A decision not to address it needs its DEC reference and retained rationale/consequences. It never counts as fulfilling the need.
5. When authorised, update the current assessment row and retain the supporting evidence and decision history in their owners. Do not copy need wording into a second maintained need register.
6. Reconcile the full relevant active-need set with exactly one current row per need before any overall statement. Report the population checked, missing or duplicate assessments, recorded unmet groups, declined work and mixed scopes. Summary counts describe recorded rows, not completeness or overall service success. Keep the two dimensions separate; do not create a completion percentage or award arbitrary partial credit.

## Result

Return the human-assessed needs, fulfilment states, separate delivery decisions and gaps requiring the team's judgement. State the exact version/scope, evidence and population limits. Arithmetic and reference checks do not constitute a human assessment.
