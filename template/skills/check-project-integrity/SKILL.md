# Check project integrity

Follow the [shared skill rules](../README.md). Use this for an explicit integrity review, repair or requested project-health assessment. Do not run a whole-project audit as a side effect of adding one record.

## Establish coverage and mode

Read the project README, AI guidance, record conventions and relevant file guides. Identify the framework version and inspect the actual files and declared external sources. Distinguish unused optional scaffold from a missing owner needed by a referenced record. A read-only review must not write. A request to check and fix authorises bounded repairs; it does not authorise new service decisions, publication or external actions.

For a complete review, inspect all active owning records and their material relationships, not merely the folder names. Establish workbook headers, tables and complete relevant rows; include narrative records and retained design versions. Report inaccessible formats, restricted sources and incomplete coverage explicitly. Do not claim to understand or verify material that could not be examined.

## Check what matters

- **Structure and schema:** expected owner locations, actual column names, usable tables and controls, working local links, and retained version paths. Use the version’s documented migrations; do not guess which conflicting schema is authoritative.
- **Identity and relationships:** unique record keys; exact allowed ID types; ID-plus-version references for journeys/prototypes; no missing targets, duplicate current scope items, self-replacements or replacement cycles. Look up reverse relationships in their owner rather than maintaining another list.
- **Completeness for use:** fields required at the current stage, applicable consent before participant research, findings based on human analysis with supporting evidence, and completed research with the team's substantive findings retained—including contrary or inconclusive learning. Check the existing protocol for known unsynthesised material. No findings is not proof that research found nothing, and populated files cannot prove every human insight was shared.
- **Knowledge and lifecycle:** proposed versus established claims, current versus superseded records, explicit replacement gaps, meaningful discarded options and reasons, conflicting source claims, and sources whose authority, currency or access is unresolved.
- **Need coverage:** every relevant active UN has an assessment or an explicit gap; no duplicate current assessment rows; evidence matches the assessed version/scope; fulfilment and delivery decision are separate; declining work is not counted as meeting a need. Check summaries against their actual population, not just their arithmetic.

## Repair within authority

Read the exact current target before writing. Preserve evidence, stable IDs and source meaning. Keep a recoverable version for removals or structural changes using the workspace’s existing method; do not scatter backup files among live records.

Safe repairs include formatting, a broken path with a uniquely established correct target, a documented column migration with data preserved, and removal of an exact redundant mirror after confirming the owning record is identical. Verify that moving or removing a duplicated link leaves the relationship represented in its owner. Do not use fuzzy matching to repair an ID.

Do not invent missing research, consent, quotations, dates, authority, relationships or decisions. Do not analyse raw research to fill a missing finding, supply a human conclusion, silently merge similar records, delete contradictory evidence, choose between disputed sources, mark a need met, or change approval/verification states. Explain these as judgement gaps with the smallest specific question or evidence needed. Continue independent safe repairs while those gaps remain.

After editing, re-read the changed files and check affected references, controls and summaries. Check that no unrelated content changed. After an uncertain write, inspect before retrying.

## Result

Lead with issues affecting a real decision: the decision or claim affected, what was checked, the unresolved gap and any safe repair. Report actual coverage and file/record references. Distinguish structural faults from content requiring human analysis and optional improvements; unused optional scaffold is not a defect count. A structural pass cannot establish that every insight was captured or every need met. Keep the result concise and use an existing owner for a meaningful unresolved issue rather than creating a new tracking system.
