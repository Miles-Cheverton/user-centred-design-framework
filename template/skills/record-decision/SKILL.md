# Record a decision

Follow the [shared skill rules](../README.md) and the [decisions guide](../../how-to/files/decisions.md).

## Procedure

1. Establish the choice actually proposed or made by people. Routine conversation, a recommendation, sarcasm and silence are not evidence of agreement. Do not infer consent from absent colleagues or invent an AI recommendation to fill the choice.
2. Check existing DEC records. Refine an unsettled proposal where appropriate; record a new decision when it replaces a materially different earlier choice.
3. Capture the choice and ID. Before recording Agreed, establish the actual decision maker, agreement and date, with enough context to understand their authority. If the source does not establish these, keep Proposed and identify the missing fact.
4. Record the team's reasons, supporting sources, meaningful alternatives and consequences. Preserve material objections and decision-changing uncertainty, without describing dissent as a personality problem. Include accepted limits or risks; do not write a persuasive rationale the team never supplied. Where consequential meaning is unclear, return that specific ambiguity to the team's existing discussion; routine authorised recording needs no new approval ritual.
5. Link affected records and any superseded decision. Preserve the previous decision and its reasoning. Update other owning records only where the authorised change and its meaning are unambiguous.

## Result

Return the DEC ID, status, actual choice and source of agreement or remaining gap. A decision to select a design or decline work is not evidence of implementation, verification or fulfilment of a user need.
