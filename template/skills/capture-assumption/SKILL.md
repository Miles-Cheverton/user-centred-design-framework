# Capture an assumption

Follow the [shared skill rules](../README.md) and the [assumptions guide](../../how-to/files/assumptions.md).

## Procedure

1. Identify the belief people have expressed and the circumstances in which the team is relying on it. Check for an existing ASM record with the same meaning. Do not invent an assumption from research interpretation or attribute an unspoken belief to a colleague.
2. Capture the ID and belief promptly. Complete consequence, check and ownership when needed to investigate or rely on the record; do not invent them just to fill cells.
3. Keep an untested belief Untested. Record a human assessment, rationale and actual date together, checking the cited EV/FND basis. Supported, Contradicted and Inconclusive describe the team's assessment in context, not universal truth. Flag a source discrepancy for people to resolve instead of deciding the status yourself.
4. Retain contradicted assumptions. When a different belief replaces one, preserve the old ID and record the exact replacements rather than rewriting the past belief.

## Result

Return the ASM ID and assessment, the proposed or completed check and any consequential unresolved gap. A request to capture an assumption does not authorise scheduling research or assigning work to another person without the relevant instruction.
