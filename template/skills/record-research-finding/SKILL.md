# Record a research finding

Follow the [shared skill rules](../README.md), [finding guide](../../how-to/files/research-findings.md) and [evidence guide](../../how-to/files/evidence.md).

## Procedure

1. Establish the conclusion humans reached through their analysis: use the supplied team conclusion, discussion or analysis notes, or existing human-produced finding. Read existing findings to avoid duplication. If only raw research is available, state that the team must analyse it first; do not generate themes, candidate findings or interpretations for approval. Continue authorised source registration or literal extraction where useful.
2. Check the conclusion against the authorised source, using its exact version and locator. If a source conflicts with the team's wording or scope, show the wording and the relevant passage together. Preserve the team's account and leave the discrepancy for human review. A source check by itself cannot establish Reviewed status.
3. Reuse matching EV records or add the literal observations needed to document the team's basis. Ensure each source resolves to an actual SES or DOC owner; register supplied material when necessary within the authorised task. Do not manufacture sessions, quotations or sample characteristics. Preserve material contrary evidence.
4. Record the team's conclusion, scope, limitations and implications in research-findings.md. Cite the analysis notes or discussion when available. Link the relevant question, session or round and exact design version. Name the affected stage or handoff where it helps the next reader locate the issue.
5. Retain all substantive human conclusions, including inconclusive results and findings that do not support the preferred design. Save when authorised. Draft means the record needs completion or review; it is not an AI-generated interpretation. Record Reviewed only when actual human review is established. Use the decision skill for the team's subsequent choice.

## Survey and Whiteboard sources

For a survey finding, use the team's analysis notes and stated conclusion, then follow the cited calculation to its exact dataset and questionnaire version. Check question IDs, included population/filter, denominator and missing-response treatment against the team's method. Flag inconsistent calculations or unsupported wording for human judgement; do not invent an interpretation, estimate a result from a chart or group free-text responses into AI-generated themes. A survey instrument is contextual material, not the response evidence.

For a finding discussed on a Whiteboard, use the team's established conclusion and retain a source reference to the relevant snapshot and area/item when accessible. A live link or visual grouping alone does not establish what people concluded. Accessible human analysis notes preserve the team's meaning when the canvas is unreadable; state that the underlying board was not independently checked. Use the existing DOC and EV routes, not a new findings or board register.

## Result

Return the FND ID, human conclusion, EV/source chain and material discrepancies needing human judgement. State which underlying sources were accessible. A summary of inaccessible research does not establish that its original material was independently checked. If analysis has not happened, report that gap without fabricating a finding.
