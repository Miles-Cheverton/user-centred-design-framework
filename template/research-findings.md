# Research findings

No findings have been recorded in this starter. The researcher and team analyse observations together and reach conclusions before documenting findings. AI may then tidy those conclusions, check them against authorised sources and populate this file faithfully; it must not originate the analysis. Copy the section below for every substantive human finding, including contradictory, unexpected and inconclusive learning. First capture the ID, conclusion and Draft status; add the basis and context before relying on it. See [the file guide](how-to/files/research-findings.md).
